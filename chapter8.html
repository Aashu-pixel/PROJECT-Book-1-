Chapter 8: Information Security and Cyber Law

Grade 11 Computer Science

Introduction

Information and communication technology has become an important part of modern society. Computers and the Internet are used for education, business, communication, banking, government services, and entertainment. Along with these benefits, the use of digital technology also creates risks related to privacy, security, cybercrime, and misuse of information.

Information security, computer ethics, cyber law, and ICT policies help individuals and organizations use digital technology safely and responsibly.

8.1 Digital Society and Computer Ethics

Digital Society

A digital society is a society in which digital technologies, computers, mobile devices, and the Internet are widely used in daily life.

In a digital society, people communicate, study, shop, work, and access government services through digital systems.

Features of a Digital Society

  • Wide use of computers and mobile devices
  • Internet-based communication
  • Online education and e-learning
  • Digital banking and online transactions
  • E-commerce and online shopping
  • Digital government services

Computer Ethics

Computer ethics refers to moral principles and rules that guide the proper and responsible use of computers and information technology.

Examples of Computer Ethics

  • Do not access another person's computer without permission.
  • Do not steal or copy someone else's work illegally.
  • Do not spread false information.
  • Respect the privacy of other users.
  • Do not create or distribute harmful software.
  • Use licensed software legally.
  • Do not use computers to harm others.

8.2 Concept of Information Security

Information security is the protection of information and information systems from unauthorized access, use, modification, destruction, or disclosure.

The main objective of information security is to protect important data from threats and unauthorized users.

Main Principles of Information Security

1. Confidentiality

Confidentiality means information should only be accessible to authorized persons.

2. Integrity

Integrity means information should remain accurate and should not be modified without authorization.

3. Availability

Availability means information and systems should be available when authorized users need them.

Importance of Information Security

  • Protects personal information
  • Protects organizational data
  • Prevents unauthorized access
  • Reduces cyber threats
  • Protects privacy
  • Ensures safe online transactions

8.3 Concept of Cybercrime

Cybercrime refers to illegal activities carried out using computers, networks, or the Internet.

Cybercriminals use digital technology to steal information, damage systems, commit fraud, or harm other individuals and organizations.

Common Types of Cybercrime

  • Hacking
  • Identity theft
  • Online fraud
  • Phishing
  • Cyberbullying
  • Software piracy
  • Spreading malware
  • Unauthorized access to computer systems

Effects of Cybercrime

  • Financial loss
  • Loss of personal information
  • Damage to computer systems
  • Loss of privacy
  • Reputation damage
  • Business disruption

8.4 Malicious Software and Spam

Malicious Software

Malicious software, commonly known as malware, is software designed to damage computers, steal information, or perform harmful activities without the user's permission.

Types of Malware

Type Description
Virus A program that attaches itself to other files and spreads when executed.
Worm A malicious program that can copy itself and spread through networks.
Trojan Horse A harmful program that appears to be useful software.
Spyware Software that secretly collects information about users.
Ransomware Malware that blocks access to data and demands payment.

Spam

Spam refers to unwanted and unnecessary electronic messages, usually sent to a large number of users.

Examples of Spam

  • Unwanted emails
  • Advertising messages
  • Fake prize messages
  • Suspicious links

Effects of Spam

  • Wastes time
  • Consumes storage space
  • May contain malware
  • Can be used for online fraud

8.5 Protection from Cybercrime

Cybercrime can be reduced by following proper security practices while using computers, mobile devices, and the Internet.

Methods of Protection

  1. Use strong and unique passwords.
  2. Do not share passwords with others.
  3. Install antivirus software.
  4. Keep software updated.
  5. Do not open suspicious emails or links.
  6. Use secure websites for online transactions.
  7. Regularly back up important data.
  8. Use two-factor authentication where possible.
  9. Do not download files from untrusted websites.
  10. Protect personal information online.

8.6 Intellectual Property Right

Intellectual Property Right (IPR) refers to the legal rights given to creators and owners of intellectual creations such as books, software, music, inventions, designs, and artistic works.

Intellectual property protects the original work of creators from unauthorized copying and use.

Types of Intellectual Property

  • Copyright
  • Patent
  • Trademark
  • Trade secret

Copyright

Copyright protects original creative works such as books, music, software, photographs, and videos.

Patent

A patent protects new inventions and gives the inventor exclusive rights for a specific period.

Trademark

A trademark is a symbol, name, or sign used to identify a product or company.

Importance of Intellectual Property Rights

  • Protects original work
  • Encourages creativity
  • Provides legal rights to creators
  • Prevents illegal copying
  • Promotes innovation

8.7 Concept of Digital Signature

A digital signature is an electronic method used to verify the authenticity and integrity of digital documents or messages.

Digital signatures use cryptographic techniques to confirm that a document has been sent by the correct person and has not been changed.

Uses of Digital Signature

  • Signing electronic documents
  • Online banking
  • E-commerce
  • Government services
  • Secure communication

Advantages of Digital Signature

  • Provides authentication
  • Ensures data integrity
  • Improves security
  • Reduces paper use
  • Saves time

8.8 Concept of Cyber Law in Nepal

Cyber law refers to laws related to the use of computers, digital communication, the Internet, and information technology.

Cyber laws help control cybercrime, electronic transactions, digital signatures, online fraud, and misuse of computer systems.

Cyber Law in Nepal

Nepal has introduced legal provisions related to electronic transactions and cyber activities. The Electronic Transactions Act (ETA), 2063 is an important law related to electronic records, digital signatures, and cybercrime in Nepal.

Objectives of Cyber Law

  • Control cybercrime
  • Recognize electronic transactions
  • Support the use of digital signatures
  • Protect electronic information
  • Provide legal punishment for cyber offences

8.9 ICT Policy in Nepal

ICT Policy refers to government policies developed to promote and manage Information and Communication Technology in the country.

ICT policies help promote the use of computers, Internet services, digital communication, e-governance, and technology-based development.

Main Objectives of ICT Policy

  • Develop ICT infrastructure
  • Increase access to technology
  • Promote digital literacy
  • Develop e-governance services
  • Promote the ICT industry
  • Improve communication systems
  • Reduce the digital divide
  • Support economic development through technology

Importance of ICT Policy

  • Promotes technological development
  • Improves government services
  • Supports digital education
  • Encourages innovation
  • Creates employment opportunities
  • Improves communication and connectivity

Difference Between Important Terms

Term Description
Information Security Protection of information from threats and unauthorized access.
Cybercrime Illegal activities performed using computers or networks.
Cyber Law Legal rules related to computers and Internet activities.
Digital Signature Electronic method used to verify digital documents.
ICT Policy Government policy for the development and management of ICT.
